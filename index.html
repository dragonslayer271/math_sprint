How to Practice

Addition Mode (0 → 100):
Starts at origin 0. The app displays a random single digit (e.g. Add 6). Calculate the new sum in your head (0 + 6 = 6) and enter 6. Next might be Add 8, so you enter 14, and so on until total exceeds 100!

Subtraction Mode (100 → 0):
Starts at origin 100. Subtract each random single digit (e.g. Sub 4 → enter 96) until you reach 0.

Timer Rule:
If the circular countdown depletes before you enter the correct sum, your progress resets back to origin! Stay fast and precise.
